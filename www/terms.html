4. Viewpoint neutrality (what we will not do)

We will not, as a matter of policy:

Community collapse is not censorship by us. If a comment is downvoted past the threshold, the interface can fold it the way Reddit folds a thread. Anyone can expand it. The words remain. Score is peer signal, not an admin ruling.

Reports exist so patterns of true illegal abuse could be studied later. In this version there is no admin takedown console. A report does not make us your moderator of convenience.

Rate limits exist to stop floods and bots. They are counted per action per short window. They are not a secret ranking of which opinions may ship.
